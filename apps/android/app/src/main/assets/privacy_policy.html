Mishran Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Mishran (“we”, “our”) operates the Mishran mobile app for ordering traditional Indian sweets. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Using the app means you accept this policy.

Data we collect

DataWhyKept
Phone number Sign-in via one-time password; order updates by SMS if you miss a push notification For the life of your account
Delivery addresses To deliver your orders; you add and remove them Until you delete them or your account
Order history (items, amounts, delivery slots) To show your orders, track deliveries, and support refunds As required by tax and accounting law
Device data (model, OS version, locale, push token) To deliver order-update notifications and keep the service working Until you sign out on that device or uninstall
App crash reports To find and fix defects (see Crashlytics below) ~90 days

We do not collect your payment card or UPI credentials, your contacts, your photos, or your location. There are no third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the app. The camera is not used in this version.

Sign-in and SMS

Sign-in uses a six-digit one-time password sent by SMS. Our SMS gateway (MSG91) receives your phone number solely to deliver that message; it processes the number under its own privacy policy as a processor. The app reads the code from the message only with the SMS Retriever API — it never asks for permission to read your messages.

Payments (Razorpay)

Checkout is processed by Razorpay, our payment gateway. When you pay:

  • The app sends Razorpay your order amount and a Razorpay order id we create.
  • You enter payment details inside Razorpay's own checkout screen; those details go to Razorpay and never touch our servers.
  • Razorpay tells us only whether the payment succeeded, its reference ids, and the payment method used. We store those references with your order.

Razorpay processes data under its privacy policy as an independent payment processor. If money leaves your account but the order fails, we refund it through Razorpay within 5–7 business days.

Push notifications

Order updates are delivered through Firebase Cloud Messaging using a push token tied to your device. You can decline or revoke the notification permission at any time in system settings; ordering works without it, and we fall back to SMS for critical updates.

Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes, a report (device model, OS version, stack trace) is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the defect. Reports contain no message content and are not used for advertising.

Permissions the app requests

  • Notifications — order updates. Optional; you can decline.
  • Biometrics — optional “quick access” sign-in. Your fingerprint or face is verified by your device; we never receive it. The sign-in key stored on the device is encrypted by your device keystore.

Data on your device

The app caches your catalog, cart, and orders on the device so it works offline. Signing out deletes this cache and your sign-in tokens from the device.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correction, or deletion of your account (subject to records we must keep for tax law) by contacting support below. Deletion requests are honored within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes are announced in the app before they take effect.
