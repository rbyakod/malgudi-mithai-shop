## Long description (4000 max)

Mishran brings the mithai shop to your phone — the sweets you grew up with, made fresh and delivered with care.

FRESH, NEVER FORGOTTEN
• Same-day delivery across Delhi NCR — kaju katli, motichoor laddoo, rasgulla and more, made the morning they arrive.
• Shelf-stable gift boxes shipped to the top 8 metros, with a promised freshness window on every box.

ORDER IN SECONDS
• Sign in with just your phone number — no passwords.
• Browse by family or dietary preference, from classic to sugar-free.
• Pay your way: UPI, cards, netbanking, or wallets via secure Razorpay checkout.

TRACK EVERY ORDER
• Live order status from kitchen to doorstep, with delivery slots you pick.
• Order updates on your home-screen widget and by push notification.
• Full order history with itemized bills — and one-tap reorder for the favorites.

MADE FOR YOU
• Available in 9 languages: English, हिन्दी, ಕನ್ನಡ, தமிழ், తెలుగు, मराठी, ગુજરાતી, বাংলা, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ.
• Works offline: your catalog, cart, and orders are ready even on a weak connection.
• Dark mode, biometric quick-access sign-in, and accessibility built in.

Have a question or a bulk/corporate order? Reach us at support@mishran.app.
